Idle Notation Calculator

Built for idle/incremental games that use big-number suffixes like K, M, B, T, AA, BB ... and beyond.

Notation reference
SuffixPower of 10
K10³ (thousand)
M10⁶ (million)
B10⁹ (billion)
T10¹² (trillion)
AA, BB, ... ZZ10¹⁵ – 10⁹⁰
AAA, BBB, ... ZZZ10⁹³ – 10¹⁶⁸
AAAA, BBBB, ...keeps going forever, +3 zeros per letter step
