# CakeFest 2024 (Jul 25, 2024) - Shipping and Scaling CakePHP in Real Environments

CakeFest 2024 was a chance to share practical lessons from building and operating CakePHP applications for scientific and engineering teams.

This session focused on what works in production: improving reliability, reducing maintenance overhead, and incrementally modernizing apps without blocking delivery.

## Talk highlights

- Progressive architecture upgrades instead of all-at-once rewrites
- Operational tooling for logs, queues, deployments, and observability
- Patterns for balancing local, on-prem, and cloud execution paths
- Practical tradeoffs for small teams supporting many applications
