# SwRI IR&D (Earth and Space): The Solar Eclipse Initiative (2024), 15-R6395

This SwRI internal R&D feature documents the 2024 Solar Eclipse Initiative project work.

## Context

The initiative supported science outreach and public engagement around the April 8, 2024 solar eclipse, including educational activities and demonstrations.

## Highlights

- Coordinated eclipse-focused science engagement activities.
- Supported hands-on outreach tools and demonstrations.
- Captured project outcomes across team members and event participation.
