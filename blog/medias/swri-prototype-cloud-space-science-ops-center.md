# SwRI IR&D (Earth and Space): A Prototype Cloud-Based Space Science Operations Center, 15-R6277

This SwRI internal R&D feature describes prototype work for a cloud-based space science operations center.

## Context

The effort explored how cloud-native services and infrastructure can support mission operations workflows with better scalability and operational flexibility.

## Highlights

- Prototyped services and cloud infrastructure for science operations.
- Evaluated architecture patterns for operational reliability and growth.
- Demonstrated a practical foundation for future operations-center capabilities.
