I was trying to watch a Spurs game over the air, and my cheap AliExpress antenna only worked well at certain angles.

I figured that I would need to point the antenna toward the TV broadcast towers. So I figured I should either get a better antenna or build some motorized rotating antenna with ESP32 electronics, automatic channel based aiming, etc.

But I didn't want to spend a lot of money on an antenna when they're supposed to be free.

Then while checking the actual OTA map using [Channel Master TV Antenna Map](https://www.channelmaster.com/pages/tv-antenna-map), I saw that almost every major local station broadcasts from the same general direction.

That's definitely on purpose and broadcast engineers had already solved this problem a long time ago.

I stuck it to a wall and now it works pretty solid.
