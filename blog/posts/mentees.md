## Workplace Mentorship Projects

### 2023 - Fortran to Julia conversion and benchmarking

I mentored one high school student through an independent-study requirement at his school. He converted Fortran code to Julia and benchmarked performance, including comparisons to Python. He presented the project at the end of the term and is now studying Computer Science at Notre Dame.

### 2024 - Genetic algorithms for Flappy Bird in Unity

I mentored one high school student who designed his own project to test different genetic machine learning algorithms and compare how they learned to play Flappy Bird in Unity. He has started college at UTSA and is back at SwRI.

### 2025 - Ultraviolet suppression testing for spaceflight hardware

I mentored two high school students in an independent study focused on researching and designing a test for ultraviolet suppression on spaceflight hardware. We later completed that test and reached out to vendors about turning it into a service we can provide to clients.
