# 7 Habits for Mental Discipline & Self-Control

Your attention and your time are your most valuable assets. If you protect those, almost everything else gets easier.

I wanted a short, practical list I can actually use, not just another motivation post. I may not execute any of this, but I still want to keep these in mind.

## 1) Reduce sugar

## 2) Drink about 3L of water daily

Your brain is roughly 75% water. Start treating it that way.

## 3) Curate your social media feed

What you consume shapes your mindset.

Garbage in, garbage out.

## 4) Meditate 10 minutes daily

Ten minutes is enough to build consistency. The goal is not perfection. The goal is training attention.

## 5) End showers with 60 seconds of cold water

Small discomfort practice builds control.

You are in control. Not your comfort and not your feelings.

## 6) Weekly 3-hour disappear block

No phone. No WiFi. No people.

Sit somewhere alone. Walk. Think.

Even if this one is hard to do every week, it is still worth treating as a priority.

## 7) Nightly reflection with 3 prompts

1. What did I do today?
2. What drained me?
3. What will I do differently tomorrow?

## Final reminder

Mental discipline is less about intensity and more about repeatable choices. Protect attention, protect time.
