## Why Aceso?

Children’s mental health is a critical issue that often goes unnoticed. It can be difficult for parents and healthcare professionals to identify and manage anxiety in children, especially when symptoms are subtle or misunderstood. Children may not have the vocabulary to express their feelings, and weekly meetings with a mental health professional may not provide enough context for the challenges children face today.

External pressures from school, parents, and insurance companies can complicate finding the best solution due to time and financial constraints, making the process more stressful for everyone, including the child. Our mission with Aceso was simple: analyze children’s anxiety and quickly devise the best path forward for their care.

Imagine a student who struggles with going to school. The parents worry they may need to quit their jobs to homeschool the child, and the child may miss out on important social interactions. With Aceso, the child's biomarkers are tracked throughout the day, helping to identify the root causes of anxiety. For example, if the child is only anxious from 7:30 to 8:00, it may indicate anxiety about the bus rather than the school day itself. Instead of drastic measures, parents could simply adjust the morning routine to drop the child off while they work on addressing the actual cause.

The rise of telehealth after the COVID-19 pandemic enables more consistent monitoring and support for children’s mental health, even when in-person visits are limited.

While we focused on anxiety, the system could be adapted to monitor other mental health conditions, such as depression or ADHD. Instead of defaulting to medication, Aceso could help identify specific triggers and patterns, allowing for more personalized and effective interventions such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) or meditation.

## The Vision

Aceso was designed as a software platform paired with a wearable device for children ages 6 to 14. The system would:

- Collect and process physiological indicators to track patterns of anxiety:
  - Heart rate
  - Skin conductance (sweat)
  - Microphone to capture tone of voice
- Analyze tone of voice using a local AI model to identify signs of anxiety without sending audio data to the cloud, ensuring privacy.
- Prioritize privacy and security, with all data stored securely on Microsoft Azure.
- Use our own hardware platform, with the option to integrate with existing wearable technology.
- Deliver results through an intuitive web application for healthcare professionals.

We aimed for the system to work quickly, provide reliable results, and remain practical for families and professionals. We envisioned an affordable rental model for the device, paired with a subscription service for the software.

## Key Benefits

- **Daily Monitoring:** Continuous data collection to support consistent care
- **Actionable Insights:** Clear, easy-to-understand reports for parents and professionals
- **Timely Alerts:** Notifications when spikes in anxiety occur
- **Security:** Private, secure storage hosted on Microsoft Azure

These features empower mental health professionals to more accurately diagnose and provide timely interventions.

## Results & Impact

Our prototype successfully collected physiological data and performed basic cycle analytics. More importantly, it demonstrated the potential of combining wearable technology and personalized healthcare to improve children’s lives. Aceso reflects not only an innovative approach to mental health care, but also a commitment to advancing mental health awareness and support for young people.
